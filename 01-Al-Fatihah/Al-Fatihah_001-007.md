1. In the Name of Allah—the Most Compassionate, Most Merciful.

`Compassionate: Feeling and showing deep care, love, and concern.
Merciful: Forgiving and showing mercy, even when punishment could be deserved.`

2. All praise is for Allah—Lord of all worlds,1

`Footnote - 1
 i.e., Lord of everything in existence including angels, humans, and animals.`
 
3. the Most Compassionate, Most Merciful, 

4. Master of the Day of Judgment. 

5. You ˹alone˺ we worship and You ˹alone˺ we ask for help. 

6. Guide us along the Straight Path, 

7. the Path of those You have blessed—not those You are displeased with, or those who are astray(Lost from the right path).1 

 `Footnote - 1
 It is customary to say “Âmîn” or “Amen” at the end of this sûrah.
 Customary means something that is usual, traditional, or commonly done by people.`
