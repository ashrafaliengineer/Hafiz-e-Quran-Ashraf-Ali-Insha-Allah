In the Name of Allah—the Most Compassionate, Most Merciful
1. Indeed, We have granted(Given or bestowed.) you ˹O Prophet˺ abundant(a lot) goodness.1
   
Footnote - 1
 This includes a heavenly river as well other favours in both worlds.
 
 Allah tells Prophet Muhammad ﷺ that He has given him Al-Kawthar, which includes immense blessings, a river in Paradise, and many other favors in this life and the Hereafter.

2. So pray and sacrifice to your Lord ˹alone˺(Only Allah, with no partners.).
   
3. Only the one who hates you is truly cut off (Separated, deprived, left without benefit.)˹from any goodness˺.
